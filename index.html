Our story

A small mill with a big promise

Serving Uppala and Kasaragod families since 2015 — honest grinding, clean machines, and nothing added.

Nashra Flour Mill has been a trusted name in Uppala since 2015. We combine time-honoured milling with modern hygiene standards to bring the freshest, most aromatic flours and spices to your kitchen.

It starts with hand-selected raw materials from suppliers we know. Every batch that leaves the mill is cleaned, dried, ground and checked by us — so what you cook with is exactly what it says on the pack.

Our process

From the field to your kitchen

Six careful steps behind every pack.

  1. Selection

    We hand-pick the finest raw materials from trusted local suppliers.

  2. Cleaning

    Multi-stage cleaning removes every impurity before grinding.

  3. Drying

    Dried to the right moisture for even grinding and longer shelf life.

  4. Grinding

    Traditional technique on modern machines, for the perfect texture.

  5. Quality check

    Every batch is checked for colour, aroma and fineness.

  6. Packing

    Sealed right away to lock in freshness and flavour.
